\section{Scope}
The following constraints apply to all documented design decisions.

\begin{table}[H]
  \caption{Design Constraints}
  \begin{tabular}{ll}
    \hline
    \textbf{Parameter} & \textbf{Value} \\
    \hline
    Transport medium & Wired Ethernet (LAN) only \\
    Number of DAQ nodes (validation testbed) & 2 (\texttt{node\_01}, \texttt{node\_02}) \\
    DAQ node MCU & STM32C5A3ZGT6 (IEEE 1588 hardware timestamping) \\
    Node software & FreeRTOS, lwIP TCP/IP stack and lwIP MQTT client \\
    Time synchronisation protocol & PTP (IEEE 1588), peer-to-peer delay mechanism \\
    Historian & InfluxDB v2 \\
    Target measurement standards & IEC 61000-4-30 Class S, IEEE 1459-2010 \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Wireless transport is specifically out of scope. The wired-only constraint eliminates RF-induced jitter as a variable in timestamp accuracy, permitting PTP synchronisation to operate within the sub-microsecond range achievable on a switched Ethernet topology. The two-node testbed reflects hardware availability at the time of validation; the architecture imposes no upper bound on the number of nodes.
