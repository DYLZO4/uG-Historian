\section{Topic Hierarchy}
\subsection{Naming Scheme}
Topics follow a four-level hierarchy:
\begin{lstlisting}
  microgrid/<node_id>/<subsystem>/<metric>
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{table}[H]
\caption{Hierarchy Description}
\centering
  \begin{tabular}{lll}
    \hline
    \textbf{Segment} & \textbf{Description} & \textbf{Values} \\
    \hline
    \texttt{node\_id} & Fixed, zero-padded node identifier & \texttt{node\_01}, \texttt{node\_02} \\
    \texttt{subsystem} & Electrical quantity category & \texttt{voltage}, \texttt{current} \\
    \texttt{metric} & Specific measured quantity & \texttt{phase\_a}, \texttt{phase\_b}, \texttt{phase\_c} \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Node identifiers are fixed strings assigned at firmware flash time (\texttt{NODE\_ID} in \texttt{app/node\_config.h}). MAC addresses and IP addresses are explicitly avoided as node identifiers as both are mutable and would invalidate historical records if hardware is replaced. Additional subsystems (for example \texttt{power} or \texttt{frequency}) can be introduced without structural change if the firmware is extended to publish derived quantities.

\subsection{Topics Published}
Each node publishes six telemetry topics:
\begin{lstlisting}
  microgrid/node_01/voltage/phase_a
  microgrid/node_01/voltage/phase_b
  microgrid/node_01/voltage/phase_c
  microgrid/node_01/current/phase_a
  microgrid/node_01/current/phase_b
  microgrid/node_01/current/phase_c
\end{lstlisting}

Optionally, when built with \texttt{MQTT\_PUBLISH\_DAQ2}, a node also publishes its full-rate binary stream on \texttt{daq2/<node\_id>/samples} (Section~\ref{sec:daq2}). This topic is deliberately outside \texttt{microgrid/\#}, so that the historian bridge, which expects JSON, does not subscribe to it.

\subsection{Wildcard Subscription Patterns}
\begin{table}[H]
  \caption{Wildcard Subscription Patterns}
  \centering
  \begin{tabular}{ll}
      \hline
      \textbf{Pattern} & \textbf{Use Case} \\
      \hline
      \texttt{microgrid/\#} & Full subscription: used by the historian bridge \\
      \texttt{microgrid/node\_01/\#} & All measurements from a single node (debugging) \\
      \texttt{microgrid/+/voltage/\#} & All voltage measurements across all nodes \\
      \texttt{daq2/+/samples} & Full-rate binary streams from all nodes \\
      \hline
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}
