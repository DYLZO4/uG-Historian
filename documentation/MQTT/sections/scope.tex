\section{Scope}
The following contraints apply to all documented design decisions.

\begin{table}[H]
  \caption{Design Constraints}
  \begin{tabular}{ll}
    \hline
    \textbf{Parameter} & \textbf{Value} \\
    \hline
    Transport Medium & Wired Ethernet (LAN) only \\
    Number of DAQ nodes (validation testbed) & 2 \\
    DAQ node MCUJ & STM32 (IEEE 1588 hardware timestamping supported) \\
    Time synchronisation protocol & PTP (IEEE 1588-2019) \\
    Historian & InfluxDB v2 \\
    Target measurement standards & IEC 61000-4-30 Class S, IEEE 1459-2010 \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Wireless transport is specifically out of scope. The wired only constraint eliminates RF-induced jitter as a variable in timestamp accuracy, permitting PTP synchronisation to operate within the sub-microsectond range achievable on a switched Ethernet topology. The two-node testbed reflects hardware availabilityat the time of validation; the architecture imposes no upper bound on the number of nodes.
